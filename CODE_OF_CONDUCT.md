# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat participants with respect, give constructive feedback and discuss ideas on their technical merits.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. Respect reasonable requests to stop unwelcome behaviour.

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Report concerns privately to **mayankmuthanna.m@gmail.com**. The maintainer will review reports and may remove inappropriate content, limit participation or exclude participants where necessary. Reports will be handled with discretion.
